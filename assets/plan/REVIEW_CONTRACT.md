# Independent acceptance

Read the exact selected candidate, relevant sources and task criteria in a separate fresh context. Builder screenshots and check results are supporting evidence, not substitutes for inspection. Record the candidate identity and actual review method.

## Common gates

1. Source fidelity: material claims map to precise sources, qualifications are local, and interpretations are distinguished from facts or examples.
2. Instructional fit: direct entry works for the named prior knowledge; the main view explains the essential relationship and a meaningful limit.
3. Reasoning: the reader can trace, predict, distinguish or locate something, rather than merely recognize terms.
4. Medium behavior: the actual delivered artifact renders and operates as intended.
5. Evidence access and preservation: links or references reach the intended sources; protected artifacts match their recorded identity.
6. Scope: outputs meet the bounded card and exclude unrelated edits or unsupported claims.

For interpretive subjects, do not require one uncontested causal account. Check whether the selected interpretation and material alternatives are represented fairly from the declared sources.

## Choose checks by medium

| Medium | Required observations for affected scope |
| --- | --- |
| Web or interactive | Actual wide and narrow layouts chosen for the audience, keyboard/pointer and focus, changed states, meaningful fallback, links and relevant runtime errors |
| Document or PDF | Rendered pages, reading order, legible figures, captions and references, page breaks and export behavior |
| Slides | Rendered slides at presentation size, narrative continuity, readable labels, source visibility and presenter notes where required |
| Diagram set | Labels and relationship semantics at intended size, legend, alternative text or equivalent explanation, navigable references |
| Workshop or lesson | Actual materials and instructions, exercise-answer alignment, facilitator assumptions; participant outcomes only if sessions occurred |

Use available appropriate tools and destination workflows. If a required observation is unavailable, record it as missing and do not claim a pass. Source review alone cannot verify rendering.

## Meaningful checks

Use a normal case and a changed or boundary case that would expose the targeted misconception. A static worked comparison is valid; do not demand an interactive control in every format. Use negative tests when they check a real contract, not to inflate assertion counts.

At pilot and final review, inspect all five mandatory depths for each in-scope subject and every newly introduced defining mechanism. Sample stable repeated layout only with a stated rationale. Do not infer acceptance of uninspected new subjects.

## Verdict

Return ACCEPT, REVISE or INCONCLUSIVE with the exact candidate, observations, instructional judgments, uncertainties and bounded repairs. Write REVIEW.md in the attempt directory. ACCEPT requires the card's mandatory evidence. Optional unperformed learner studies remain explicit limits.

Record participant comprehension and timed rehearsal separately. If the application makes either a release requirement, it must actually occur before closure; otherwise do not pretend it was measured.

A repair goes back to the owning builder item. Re-review affected behavior and invalidate dependent evidence when the candidate changed materially. Do not edit a defect and certify your own repair.

## Mandatory final depth gate

Every build must include a D5 detailed visualization or map for each in-scope subject. A bibliography, source index, prose explanation or generic overview alone fails this gate. D1-D4 explanations are also mandatory; each must pass its distinct audience contract.

Inspect the actual map against its scope and source inventory. Trace a representative relationship or path, verify precise supporting evidence, examine a boundary or uncertain connection, and check labels, legend, grouping, accessibility and legibility in the delivered medium. An existing map is not accepted merely because it exists.

Pilot acceptance, full-scope acceptance and closure require this gate. Evaluate-only assignments report a missing map as a gap without building one unless requested.

## Complete five depth acceptance

For every in-scope subject, verify D1, D2, D3, D4 and D5 against their separate learning outcomes, exact destinations, candidate identities and review evidence. Missing or unaccepted depth coverage blocks integration, pilot/final acceptance and closure.

Shared files are allowed; merely repeating the same content under five headings does not satisfy distinct explanations. One-depth sessions and audience-specific companions are partial work, not substitutes for the complete five-depth deliverable. Evaluation-only work reports gaps without claiming the missing artifacts were built.
