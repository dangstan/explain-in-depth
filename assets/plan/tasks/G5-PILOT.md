## Required context

Accepted pilot cards and exact candidate, pilot subject brief, acceptance matrix and output manifest.

## Owned deliverables

- This item's REVIEW.md and reviewer evidence

## Steps

1. Verify all concrete pilot dependencies and identify the exact integrated candidate.
2. Inspect all five mandatory depths as a direct entrant and then as a connected sequence.
3. Use teach-back and an unfamiliar variation to identify likely misconceptions; distinguish this expert exercise from a participant study.
4. Check source support, omitted detail, evidence access, consistency and format behavior.
5. Record ACCEPT, REVISE or INCONCLUSIVE. Send material repairs to their original items and require fresh review; do not repair and certify.

## Deliverables

Independent pilot verdict with observed evidence, instructional judgments and bounded repairs.

## Acceptance

- All five mandatory purposes pass their distinct contracts separately and together for the named readers. The actual D5 detailed map passes the mandatory final depth gate; a source list alone fails.
- Shared design choices are ready to reuse or have specific defects.
- ACCEPT allows the next subject; it does not certify unbuilt subjects.
- No extra reviewer-of-reviewer is required for this review-only card.
